cayleypy.models.MlpModel

class cayleypy.models.MlpModel(config: ModelConfig)[source]

Multi-layer perceptron model.

Consumes one-hot encoded states, applies hidden layers described by layers_sizes (each of them being Linear+LayerNorm+ReLU), then a linear output layer with n_outputs neurons.

Output has shape [n_states] when n_outputs == 1, and shape [n_states, n_outputs] otherwise.
